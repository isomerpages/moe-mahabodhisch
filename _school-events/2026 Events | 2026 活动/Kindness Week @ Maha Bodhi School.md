<p>As part of Kindness Week on 14 and 15 July, Maha Bodhians paid tribute
to our non-teaching staff who work tirelessly behind the scenes to keep
our school clean, safe and welcoming every day.</p>
<p>Through meaningful acts of appreciation, students designed gift bags,
made handmade sponge bears, penned heartfelt thank-you notes and contributed
to a Gratitude Wall filled with messages of thanks. These simple yet thoughtful
gestures reminded us that kindness begins with noticing the people around
us and valuing their contributions. Together, we celebrate the quiet dedication
of our non-teaching staff and thank them for making Maha Bodhi School a
place where every child can learn, grow and belong.</p>
<p>为配合菩提学校的行善周（Kindness Week），在7月14日和15日，菩提学子们齐心向我们的非教职人员致以最诚挚的敬意。他们日复一日，在幕后默默付出，让我们的校园日日整洁、时时安全、刻刻温馨。</p>
<p>通过一系列充满意义的感恩活动，同学们亲手设计礼品袋，制作海绵熊，书写真心实意的感谢信，并在感恩墙上留下一句句温暖的话语。这些看似简单却饱含心意的举动，提醒着我们：友善，始于关注身边的人，始于珍视他们默默的付出。让我们共同感谢非教职人员的无私奉献，正是因为有你们，菩提学校才能成为每个孩子学习、成长的地方，是他们真正的家园。</p>
