<h4><strong><u>Vesak Day Celebrations @ Maha Bodhi School 2026</u></strong></h4>
<p>Maha Bodhi School commemorated Vesak Day 2026 through a vibrant series
of activities that brought students and staff together in reflection, celebration
and learning.</p>
<p>The Vesak Day celebrations began with the Vesak Day Observance Ceremony
on 15 May 2026, where students and staff reflected on the values of gratitude,
compassion and mindfulness. The Observance Ceremony included the offering
of flowers to Lord Buddha by Venerable Seck Kwang Phing and the School
Leaders, as well as the symbolic Bathing of the Buddha Ceremony. This was
followed by the recitation of the Heart Sutra by Venerable Seck Kwang Phing
and a sharing on the significance of Vesak Day.</p>
<p>From 15 to 26 May 2026, Buddhist staff and students participated in the
Bathing of Lord Buddha Ceremony, providing opportunities for quiet reflection
and the cultivation of mindfulness and gratitude.</p>
<p>As part of the school’s Vesak Day celebrations, Primary 1 to Primary 6
students took part in the Vesak Day Drawing and Colouring Competition —
a Character and Citizenship Education (Mother Tongue) initiative that promotes
the appreciation of values through creative expression. Through their artwork,
students expressed school values such as respect, resilience, integrity,
compassion and excellence.</p>
<p>Complementing these activities, students also explored the origins and
traditions of Vesak Day (卫塞节的由来和习俗) through Bodhi DNA (菩提 DNA), the school’s
signature SAP programme that promotes an appreciation of Chinese culture
and heritage. This pre-assembly programme deepened students’ understanding
of the significance of Vesak Day.</p>
<p>Through these meaningful Vesak Day learning experiences, students learnt
to embody values of gratitude, kindness and compassion in their daily lives,
reflecting Maha Bodhi School’s holistic, balanced and values-driven culture
anchored in character, mindfulness and care for others.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<h4><strong>卫塞节庆典 2026</strong></h4>
<p>2026年，菩提学校通过一系列丰富而有意义的活动庆祝卫塞节，让师生们在学习、体验与反思中，加深对感恩、慈悲与正念价值观的理解。</p>
<p>卫塞节庆典于2026年5月15日正式展开，学校举行了庄严而意义深远的卫塞节庆典仪式。师生们齐聚一堂，共同学习与感悟感恩、慈悲与正念的重要价值。庆典仪式包括由释广品法师与学校领导向佛陀献花，以及象征净化身心的浴佛仪式。随后，释广品法师诵念《心经》，并分享卫塞节的意义。</p>
<p>配合此次庆典，学校也于2026年5月15日至26日期间安排佛教师生参与浴佛仪式，让大家在过程中静心反思，并培养感恩与正念之心。</p>
<p>作为学校卫塞节庆祝活动的一部分，小一至小六学生也参与了“卫塞节绘画与填色比赛”。这项属于品格与公民教育（母语）的活动，通过艺术创作，引导学生表达对价值观的理解与体会。学生们在作品中展现了尊重、坚毅、正直、慈悲与追求卓越等学校价值观。</p>
<p>此外，学生们也通过学校特色 SAP 课程——“菩提 DNA（菩提 DNA）”的晨会活动，学习《卫塞节的由来和习俗》，进一步了解卫塞节的历史背景与文化意义，加深对中华文化与佛教传统的认识与欣赏。</p>
<p>通过这一系列富有意义的卫塞节学习体验，学生们学习在日常生活中实践感恩、善良与慈悲，体现了菩提学校以品格培养、正念教育及关爱他人为核心、重视全人发展与价值观教育的校园文化。</p>
