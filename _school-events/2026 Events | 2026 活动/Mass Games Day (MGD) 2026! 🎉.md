<h3><strong>Mass Games Day (MGD) 2026</strong></h3>
<p>On 6 August 2026, our P3, P4 and P5 pupils took part in station games
and went head-to-head in Badminton, Freeze Ball, and Dribble Ball!</p>
<p>We are so proud of every pupil for giving their best, displaying sportsmanship,
and having lots of fun!</p>
<p>Huge thanks to our awesome Parent Volunteers for refereeing and supporting
our PE teachers to make MGD 2026 a success! 🙌</p>
<p>See you at MGD 2027!</p>
<h3><strong>2026年菩提游戏日（Mass Games Day，MGD）圆满落幕！🎉</strong></h3>
<p>2026年8月6日，我们三、四和五年级的学生参与了各项趣味站点游戏，并在羽毛球、Freeze Ball（冻结球）和Dribble Ball（运球）项目中展开精彩对决！</p>
<p>我们为每一位学生感到骄傲！大家全力以赴，展现了良好的体育精神，同时也享受了运动带来的欢乐！</p>
<p>特别感谢抽空前来支援这项活动的家长志工们！🙌 感谢大家不只来担任裁判，还协助体育老师进行活动，让MGD 2026顺利圆满举行！</p>
<p>2026年的MGD已圆满落幕，我们期待2027年再相见！🎉</p>
