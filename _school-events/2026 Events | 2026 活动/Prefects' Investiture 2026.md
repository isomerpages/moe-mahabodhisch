<h4><strong>Prefects' Investiture 2026</strong></h4>
<p>On 28 July 2026, Maha Bodhi School held its Prefects' Investiture to formally
induct our newly appointed prefects into the Prefectorial Board. The ceremony
marked the beginning of their leadership journey and reminded them of their
responsibility to serve the school community with dedication, integrity
and humility.</p>
<p>During the ceremony, the Executive Committee members of the Prefectorial
Board received their prefect badges from Venerable Seck Kwang Phing and
Mrs See. Together with their fellow prefects, they also took the Prefects'
Pledge, affirming their commitment to uphold the school values and lead
by example.</p>
<p>As they embark on this new chapter, we wish our prefects every success
as they strive to make a positive difference in the lives of others and
the Maha Bodhi community.</p>
<h4><strong>2026年学生领袖——学长就任典礼</strong></h4>
<p>2026年7月28日，菩提学校举行了学长就任典礼，正式欢迎新任学长加入学长委员会。此次典礼标志着他们领导历程的开始，也提醒他们肩负着以奉献、正直与谦逊的态度服务学校社群的责任。</p>
<p>在典礼上，学长委员会的执行委员会成员，从德高望重的广品法师和校长手中接过学长徽章。他们也与其他学长一同宣读《学长誓词》，承诺维护学校价值观，并以身作则，发挥领导作用。</p>
<p>在开启这一崭新篇章之际，我们祝愿所有学长在发挥为他人及菩提学校社群带来积极影响的过程中取得圆满成功。</p>
