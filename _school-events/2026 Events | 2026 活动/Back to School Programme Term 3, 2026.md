<p><strong>Back-to-School Programme (Term 3, 2026)</strong>
</p>
<p>Our Back-to-School Programme welcomed pupils back to school through meaningful
opportunities to reconnect with friends and strengthen relationships. During <strong>Breakfast with Buddy</strong>,
Primary 1 pupils bonded with their Primary 4 buddies, while Primary 2 pupils
spent quality time with their Primary 5 buddies over breakfast, sharing
conversations and holiday experiences.</p>
<p>Primary 3 and Primary 6 buddy classes also came together for <strong>Buddy Interaction Time</strong>,
where they enjoyed playing games and interacting in their classrooms. Across
the school, pupils also participated in <strong>class bonding activities</strong> that
fostered a positive classroom climate, strengthened friendships and set
a warm, welcoming tone for the new school term.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>2026年第三学段迎新活动</strong>
</p>
<p>为迎接新学段开学，学校举办了一系列富有意义的迎新活动，让同学们在轻松愉快的氛围中重新与朋友相聚，并进一步增进彼此之间的友谊。在和伙伴一起享用早餐的活动中，一年级学生与四年级伙伴共进早餐，二年级学生则与五年级伙伴一同享用早餐，在轻松交流中分享假期点滴，增进彼此认识与情谊。</p>
<p>与此同时，三年级与六年级伙伴班也进行了伙伴互动活动。同学们在课室内一起玩游戏、互动交流，共度欢乐的学习时光。此外，全校各班也开展了班级凝聚活动，营造积极正向的班级氛围，促进同学之间的友谊，并为新学段营造温馨、融洽的学习氛围，为新学段揭开美好的序幕。</p>
