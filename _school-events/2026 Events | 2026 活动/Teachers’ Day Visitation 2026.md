<h4>The school will be celebrating Teachers' Day on 3 September.</h4>
<h4>We warmly welcome our former students to visit their teachers between 11.00 a.m. and 12.30 p.m. Students who are currently studying in secondary schools or junior colleges are reminded to wear their current school uniform during their visit.</h4>
<h4>To help manage the expected crowd, we strongly encourage all former students to inform their teachers in advance if they plan to visit. Visitors are also reminded to observe school rules and follow the instructions given by our school staff.</h4>
<h4>Thank you.</h4>
